The Little Seed

Written by Story Garden

Once upon a time, a tiny seed rested beneath the soft and warm soil.

It dreamed of growing into a beautiful tree, but it was afraid of the dark.

One morning, gentle rain touched the soil. The little seed slowly began to grow.

Days passed. The tiny seed became a small plant, and the plant grew into a strong tree.

The little seed finally understood that every great journey begins with a small step.

From that day onward, the tree provided shade to birds, shelter to animals, and happiness to everyone who rested beneath it.

The little seed was no longer afraid. It had become something wonderful.

Moral: Great things take time. Be patient, believe in yourself, and keep growing.
